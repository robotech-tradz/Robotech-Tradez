Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pricing, enquiries and service delivery.

Are the listed prices final?

Prices marked as fixed apply only to the scope stated. Custom work, additional features, subscriptions and third-party charges may be quoted separately. Confirm the final scope and amount in writing before payment.

How do I request a custom quotation?

Use the Google Form or contact the team through WhatsApp or Telegram. Include your requirements, platform, desired features and timeline so the scope can be assessed.

Do you guarantee trading profits or signals?

No. Trading and investing involve risk. Software, indicators and technical tools do not guarantee profits or future results. Make independent decisions and assess your risk tolerance.

How can I confirm a payment?

Follow the payment instructions provided with your confirmed quotation, then share the transaction reference through an official communication channel. Payment confirmation is manual unless a verified payment integration is added.

Can I request a service that is not listed?

Yes. Send your requirement using the enquiry form. Availability, feasibility, delivery timeline and pricing will be confirmed after review.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you’re trying to build or solve. We’ll help clarify the scope and next steps.
